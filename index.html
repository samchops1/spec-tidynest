Our cleaning services

Regular, deep, move-in, and move-out.

We offer a wide range of home cleaning services. Our services are affordable and flexible, and we strive to provide the best value for your money. Call or email us for a quote.

Standard maintenance

Keep your home consistently fresh with our regular maintenance cleaning.

  • Kitchen — counters, cabinets, sink, microwave, and all appliances; vacuum/sweep and mop floors
  • Bathrooms — toilets, tubs/showers, counters, and mirrors; vacuum/sweep and mop floors
  • Bedrooms — dust and wipe down surfaces; vacuum/sweep and mop floors
  • Common areas — dust and wipe down surfaces, tidy up, vacuum/sweep and mop floors

Call or email for a quote

Deep cleaning

Deep cleaning goes beyond the surface — we scrub, shine, and refresh every corner. When a regular cleaning isn’t enough, our deep clean gets the job done.

  • Kitchen — inside and outside of appliances, counters, cabinets, sink, and backsplash; vacuum/sweep and mop floors
  • Bathrooms — toilets, tubs/showers, sinks, counters, mirrors, tile, and grout; vacuum/sweep and mop floors
  • Bedrooms — all surfaces, baseboards, doors, and trim; vacuum, sweep, and mop
